Chariots of Grace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Chariots of Grace is a free educational game published by GiggleByte. It is made for players of all ages, including children. This policy applies to the website at gigglebyte-hub.github.io/Chariots-of-Grace and to the Chariots of Grace apps on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. The only exception is the limited order information Google Play or Apple may share if you choose to donate (see below).

What stays on your device

Your journey progress and best score are is saved in your browser's or phone's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data.

Offline files

The game saves a copy of its own pages, pictures and code on your device so it can work without internet. These files contain no personal information.

Links to other websites

The website version includes a link to Ko-fi for optional donations, and a link to the game's source code on GitHub. If you follow those links, you leave the game, and those websites' own privacy policies apply. The Google Play and Apple apps do not link to Ko-fi.

Optional donations in the Google Play and Apple apps

The Google Play and Apple App Store apps offer optional one-time donations. They unlock nothing in the game, and a grown-up check appears before each one. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play (under Google's privacy policy) or by Apple (under Apple's privacy policy). We never see card or bank details. Google or Apple may share limited order information with us, such as the order number, date, amount and country, which we use only for accounting.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. The game contains no chat, no messaging and no user-generated content. Nothing in the game can be bought; the only purchases are the optional donations described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/GiggleByte-Hub/Chariots-of-Grace/issues.
