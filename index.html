The Ten Commandments

Exodus 20:1–17 (KJV). Your journey record appears beside each one as you play.

    The greatest commandments

    Jesus summed up the whole law in two: love God with all your heart, soul and mind — and love your neighbour as yourself (Matthew 22:37–39). The first four commandments teach love for God; the last six teach love for people.

    How to play

    1. 🛞 Travel by chariot through 12 stops — one Bible story for each of the Ten Commandments, plus a story of forgiveness and a story of giving.
    2. 📖 Read the story, then face a life choice from today's world that tests the same commandment.
    3. ⚖️ Choose. Faithful choices earn a 👑 crown, raise your ❤️ virtue and ✝️ faith, and earn 🪙 talents.
    4. 🪨 Wrong choices add a burden. But the road home is always open: choose to repent like the prodigal son and your burden is lifted. Forgiving someone else lifts a burden too.
    5. 🎲 Cast the lot for a Moment of Grace after each stop — manna, ravens, angels, a double portion, or a trial. Strong faith (70+) improves your roll.
    6. 👼 Angels can show you the wise path on a hard choice, and stand guard during a trial.
    7. 🔥 Upgrade your chariot with talents, from a shepherd's cart all the way to a chariot of fire.
    8. 🪑 Reach the throne room at the end, lay down your burdens, and cast your crowns before the throne.

    A note on grace

    The dice add surprise — but in the game, as in the Bible, forgiveness is never left to chance. Whenever you repent, you're forgiven, every time (1 John 1:9). The "Moments of Grace" are unexpected blessings along the road, reflecting the idea that "time and chance happeneth to them all" (Ecclesiastes 9:11) while God remains in control (Proverbs 16:33).
